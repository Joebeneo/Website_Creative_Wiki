Backstory & Lore

Nobody knows exactly where Cleo is from, and she prefers it that way. She gives a different answer every time someone asks — a different city, a different country, sometimes a different planet. The inconsistency isn't sloppy. It's intentional. A person without a traceable past is a person who's very hard to find.

What is known: she grew up moving constantly, never staying anywhere long enough to put down roots. She learned early that people talk freely when they think you're passing through, and she developed an almost supernatural ability to listen — really listen — to what people say, what they don't say, and what they wish they hadn't said.

The Collection

Cleo keeps a small leather-bound book that she calls "the ledger." It contains no names, no dates — just cryptic shorthand that only she can read. She's been filling it since she was sixteen. She describes it as a collection of insurance policies. Others might call them secrets.

She doesn't use what she knows maliciously. Mostly. But she does use it to navigate, to trade, to protect herself and the rare few people she decides are worth protecting. It's a cold calculus dressed up as pragmatism, and she's aware of that.

Meeting Nova

Cleo met Nova in a rainstorm when they both ducked under the same awning outside a parts shop. Nova, characteristically, tried to explain to Cleo why the rain's angle of fall indicated an approaching pressure system. Cleo, characteristically, pointed out that she didn't care and offered to share an umbrella.

They've been unlikely friends ever since. Nova is one of very few people Cleo trusts — not because Nova is naive, but because Nova has never once tried to figure out what Cleo is hiding. That, to Cleo, is an almost unbearable kindness.

Self-Sufficient

Cleo comes across as cool, aloof, and mildly threatening — which is largely intentional. She reads a room in seconds, calibrates her words for maximum effect, and very rarely says what she actually means on the first try. But underneath the performance is someone who cares far too much and is furious about it. She would never admit this. Don't tell her you noticed.
